## Measured 2026-08-31 — what SP does on this box

Operator moved the S-4000S's REAC Mode switch M -> S -> SP, power-cycling only for the last.

**On SP the box TRANSMITS NOTHING on this port.** Link stays up at 100 Mb/s full duplex, and an
8 s tcpdump sees only OUR frames:

    3000  00:14:5c:9b:28:2d : 1492    <- us, 40ch master downstream
       0  00:40:ab:c4:08:bc           <- the box: silent

The daemon's own message changes accordingly — from `box present, not joining` (M mode: frames
arriving, no JOIN) to **`no sustained presence`** with `rx_box_frames` FROZEN at 128 446 627.

SP is SPLIT: the box divides its I/O across TWO REAC ports so two consoles (FOH and monitors)
can share one stagebox. With one cable, in the port SP assigns to the other half, the box is
simply not there from this segment's view. Coherent behaviour, not a fault — but **a box on SP
needs both ports patched**, and a single-cable rig cannot use SP at all.

## And it confirms the boot-latch, in both directions

- M -> S with NO power cycle: nothing changed. The box kept mastering, `rx_joins` frozen at 482.
- S -> SP WITH a power cycle: behaviour changed completely.

**The REAC Mode switch is read at BOOT and never re-read.** That is why every remedy this project
writes says "set it to S and power-cycle" — the power-cycle is required, not caution.

## Recovery, and what it cost

Getting the box back took several attempts, and each failed attempt is a measurement:

1. M -> S, no power cycle: no change (the box kept mastering).
2. S -> SP, power cycled: box went silent on this port.
3. SP -> S, power cycled: STILL silent — the switch was not fully seated, and a partial seat
   behaves exactly like SP.
4. S fully seated, power cycled: enrolled, `reac-disco: model=s4000s (ours)`, and a physical
   loopback measured 110 dB of separation.

**Throughout 2 and 3 the REAC LED was lit and STEADY — identical to synched.** The lamp reports
the box's own view of its link, not whether it is transmitting to us. Three indicators disagreed
with the wire on this rig today: the enrolment log on the USB NIC, a deaf pw-record probe, and
this LED. The wire was right every time.

**And the daemon never saw the link cycle.** The kernel logged `Link status is: 0` then `1` for
every power cycle; reac-pw logged nothing and stayed in PROBING (issue #95, no link-state watch).
A box only cold-connects on link-up, so a master that cannot see a link-up cannot re-arm for it —
which is why a daemon RESTART was needed after a box reboot that should have been sufficient on
its own. That is the sharpest case yet for fixing #95.


## Neither end pairs with a box on M — tried BOTH, 2026-08-31

With the S-4000S on M, both roles were configured against it and neither established:

| we ran as | we transmitted | the box did |
|---|---|---|
| MASTER (our default) | 1492 master downstream, broadcast | broadcast only, `rx_joins=0` — never cold-connected |
| SLAVE 16-ch | 628 upstream, UNICAST to its MAC | broadcast only, never granted |
| SLAVE 32-ch | 1204 upstream, UNICAST to its MAC | broadcast only, never granted |

Our side behaved correctly in every arm — the slave FSM reached `COLDCONNECT` and emitted the
right width to the right address, and `reac_slave.h` is explicit that slave role is still the
MIXER ("the mixer's own input channels ... only WHO drives the handshake + the clock differs").

**The box's own frames are the interesting part: it broadcasts 1206/1204 — its UPSTREAM
geometry, what a box sends TO a mixer.** It never emits master-downstream frames. So on M it is
not running a console's send pattern; it sends box-shaped traffic, unsolicited and unaddressed.
That is consistent with it neither accepting a grant nor issuing one.

**Open question for someone who knows the hardware:** what M actually means on an S-4000S. It may
not be "act as a console" — it could be a clock-source selection, or a split/standalone mode,
which would explain a box that talks like a box but pairs with nothing. Everything above is what
the wire shows; the box's intent is not established.

Practically: **a box on M is unusable from this console in any configuration.** Set the switch to
S, fully seated, and power-cycle.
