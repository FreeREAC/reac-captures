# Two hazards found while running it (both operational, both go in docs/ETF-PACING.md):
# 1. A thread-backend daemon under a leftover etf qdisc transmits NOTHING: with skip_sock_check the qdisc

#    for the window). The qdisc and the backend are one setting; never leave one without the other.
# 2. `tc qdisc replace` fails on an existing etf qdisc ("Change operation not supported"); use del + add.
